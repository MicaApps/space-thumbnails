# Privacy Policy for Space Thumbnails

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

Space Thumbnails is a local Windows utility designed to generate thumbnails for various file formats. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Data Collection and Processing
Space Thumbnails is a **fully local application**.
- **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage statistics, or telemetry data.
- **Local Processing Only**: All file processing and thumbnail generation happen entirely on your local machine. No data is ever uploaded to a server.
- **No Network Communication**: The core functionality of Space Thumbnails does not require or use an internet connection.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Space Thumbnails does not integrate with any third-party tracking or analytics services.

## 3. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue on the GitHub repository.
